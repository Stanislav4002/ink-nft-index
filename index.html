# Collection Holders ↓ Supply Transfers Turnover Spread

Source: the Ink block explorer's public API (explorer.inkonchain.com/api/v2). Nothing is cached and nothing is stored — your browser queries the chain directly every time this page loads, so every figure here can be checked against the explorer. New collections show up on their own; no list is maintained by hand.

What the numbers are. Holders is distinct wallets holding at least one token. Supply is total minted. Spread is holders divided by supply — how widely a collection is actually distributed. A collection of 2,000 pieces held by 1,700 wallets is spread; the same 2,000 held by 150 wallets is not, and that is usually a more honest signal than floor price.

Verified is a human judgement, not a chain fact. Nothing on-chain marks a contract as the official one, and on Ink several contracts share a well-known collection's name. So the Verified list is kept by hand, in a plain file anyone can read, and a verified contract is never hidden by any of the filters. Everything else stays reachable under All — the copies are not deleted, only moved out of the way.

Turnover ranks movement, not standing. It is transfers divided by supply: how often the average token has changed hands. A collection sitting near 1× was minted and then never moved again, which is the clearest sign of a drop nobody wanted. But a collection people buy and hold scores low too, so the most respected names on a chain are often not the busiest ones. Sort by holders for standing and by turnover for movement — neither column alone is a ranking of what matters. Where the explorer contradicts its own supply figure, turnover is shown with a ~ and is never used to hide a collection.

What they are not. Floor prices and trading volume live on marketplaces, not on‑chain, so they are deliberately absent rather than estimated. The collection / utility split is a heuristic: every row shows the reason it was classified, and the Utility tab shows everything filtered out, so nothing hides behind the judgement.
